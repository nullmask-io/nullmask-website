# Project Roadmap

## NullMask — Product Roadmap

NullMask is being built as a privacy layer that works across wallets, applications and chains without requiring users to abandon their existing Web3 setup.

The roadmap begins with the Ethereum mainnet release and expands progressively into incentives, multichain infrastructure, cross-chain privacy and developer integrations.

> **Note:** Roadmap dates are current targets and may change based on development, testing, audits and security requirements.

### Phase 1 — Mainnet Launch

#### Ethereum Mainnet Launch

NullMask goes live on Ethereum mainnet. Users can shield assets into the private pool and transact without exposing their on-chain history.

**ETA: 1st October 2026**

#### Advanced Unshielding

A more flexible way to exit the shielded pool, designed to provide stronger unlinkability between deposit and withdrawal.

Advanced Unshielding will be announced as part of the initial release.

**ETA: 1st October 2026**

***

### Phase 2 — Community & Incentives

#### Points System

Users earn points for shielding, transacting and other protocol activity.

The points system is designed to reward early participation, encourage protocol usage and support future community incentive programs.

**ETA: 1st Week of October 2026**

***

### Phase 3 — Multichain Expansion & Security

#### BNB Chain, Base & Robinhood Chain Support

NullMask begins its multichain expansion across additional EVM ecosystems.

At least one additional EVM network is targeted for deployment during this phase, extending NullMask's privacy infrastructure to new applications, liquidity and user bases.

**ETA: 1st - 2nd  Week of October 2026**

#### Cross-Chain Bridge

Private asset transfers between supported networks, allowing liquidity to move across chains without requiring users to leave the NullMask privacy layer.

**ETA: October 2026**

#### Unshield Directly to ZEC Wallets

Users will be able to withdraw assets from NullMask directly into a Zcash wallet.

This connects NullMask with the Zcash ecosystem and provides users with a native privacy-preserving exit route.

**ETA: October 2026**

***

### Phase 4 — Ecosystem & Adoption

#### NullMask SDK

A developer SDK enabling wallets, dApps, exchanges and other services to integrate NullMask privacy functionality directly into their products.

The SDK will form a core part of NullMask's partnership and business-development strategy, allowing privacy functionality to be distributed through products users already use rather than requiring every user to interact with NullMask directly.

**ETA: Q4 2026**

#### Private Perpetuals

Perpetual futures trading from a shielded balance, allowing traders to keep their positions, size and strategy confidential.

**ETA: Q4 2026**

#### Solana Integration

NullMask expands beyond EVM networks to Solana, bringing shielded transactions to the Solana ecosystem.

The integration also aligns NullMask's multichain privacy infrastructure with the home chain of the **MASK token**.

**ETA: Q4 2026**

***

### The Direction

The roadmap moves NullMask through four clear stages:

**Private transactions → Incentivised adoption → Multichain privacy → Privacy infrastructure**

The end goal is broader than a single privacy application.

**NullMask is building a privacy layer that wallets, applications and users can access across Web3.**
