# Deposit

A deposit moves ETH or a supported token from your wallet into your private balance.

## Steps

1. Open **Shield** in the app and choose the asset and the amount.
2. Check the quote. It shows what you get in the pool and every fee.
3. Press **Check and deposit**. The app checks the deposit first. Nothing leaves your wallet yet.
4. For a deposit of $1,000 or more, your wallet asks for one more signature. It is free: no transaction and no gas.
5. Confirm the deposit in your wallet. For a token, your wallet first asks you to approve it.
6. Wait for the deposit to be credited. The app shows its status.

## What the quote shows

- **In the pool you get:** the exact amount credited to your private balance.
- **Deposit fee:** 0.5% of the amount.
- **Withdrawal fee (prepaid):** the 0.5% fee of a later withdrawal, paid now. You can turn this off.
- **To your Gas:** ETH added to your [Gas balance](gas.md) for later network fees. You can turn this off.
- **Network & service fee:** ETH held for the gas of crediting your deposit. The unused part comes back to you.
- **Your wallet gas:** the gas your wallet pays to send the deposit.
- **Total from your wallet.**

All fees are added on top, so the amount you choose is the amount you get in the pool.

## How long it takes

Most deposits are credited within minutes. Some wait longer. See [Deposit checks](deposit-checks.md).

## Minimum amounts

Each asset has a minimum deposit. See [Networks and addresses](networks.md).

> Deposit only from a wallet you control. A deposit that is not accepted goes back to the address it came from, so never deposit from an exchange.
