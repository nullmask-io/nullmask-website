## The Nullmask network in your wallet

The app adds the Nullmask network to your wallet for you. Do not add it by hand from another source. Its link is personal: it lets your wallet read your private balance, so do not share it.

Your wallet shows your private balance on this network. Your regular balance stays on Ethereum or Base as before.

> Always check the pool address before you deposit. Nullmask will never ask you to send funds to any other address.
