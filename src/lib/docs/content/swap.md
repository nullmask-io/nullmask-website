# Swap

A swap exchanges one asset for another inside your private balance. The blockchain shows the assets and the amounts, but not who made the swap.

| Network | Pairs | Exchange |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ethereum | ETH and USDT | Uniswap |
| Base | ETH and USDC | Aerodrome |

## Steps

1. Open **Swap** and choose the assets and the amount.
2. Choose **Standard amount** or **Any amount**. Standard amounts are harder to spot on-chain.
3. Set your slippage tolerance and check the quote.
4. Confirm in your wallet.

## Fees

Nullmask takes no protocol fee on swaps. The exchange's pool fee is already in the rate, and you pay a network fee in ETH from your private balance. See [Fees](fees.md).
