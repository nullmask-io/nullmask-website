# Send privately

A private transfer moves funds from your private balance to another Nullmask account. The blockchain does not show the sender, the recipient or the amount.

## Steps

1. Open **Send** and enter the recipient's wallet address and the amount.
2. The app shows whether this is a private transfer or a withdrawal.
3. Confirm in your wallet.

## Private transfer or withdrawal

- If the recipient has a Nullmask account, the transfer is private.
- If the recipient has never used Nullmask, the same action is a [withdrawal](withdraw.md): the recipient address, the asset and the amount are public. The app says so before you confirm.

## Fees

A private transfer has no protocol fee. You pay only the network fee, in ETH from your private balance. See [Fees](fees.md).
