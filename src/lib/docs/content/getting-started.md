# Get started

You need a self-custody wallet such as MetaMask or Rabby, and some ETH for gas. A wallet controlled by a smart contract cannot hold a private balance.

## 1. Connect your wallet

Open [app.nullmask.io](https://app.nullmask.io) for Ethereum or [base.nullmask.io](https://base.nullmask.io) for Base, and connect your wallet.

## 2. Create your account keys

Your wallet asks you to sign a message from Nullmask. It creates the keys of your Nullmask account.

- It is free: no transaction and no gas.
- It moves no funds and approves nothing.
- The same wallet always gives the same keys, on any device.

## 3. Add the Nullmask network

The app asks your wallet to add a network called **Nullmask VPN on Ethereum** (or **on Base**). Approve it. Your private balance is shown on this network. See [Networks and addresses](networks.md).

## 4. Register your account

Your account is registered on-chain so that others can send to you privately. You can register on its own, or together with your first deposit. Registration is a transaction from your wallet, and you pay its gas.

## 5. Make your first deposit

Choose an asset and an amount and press **Check and deposit**. See [Deposit](deposit.md).
