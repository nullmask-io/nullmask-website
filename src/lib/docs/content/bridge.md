# Bridge

Bridge moves assets from your wallet to another network or another asset. It is carried out by NEAR Intents, a third-party service, and covers dozens of networks.

Bridge sends from your wallet, not from your private balance.

## Steps

1. Open **Bridge** and choose what you send and what you receive.
2. Enter the amount and the recipient address. Check the quote: it shows what you receive, the minimum you receive and the estimated time.
3. Press **Get deposit address**.
4. Send the exact amount from your wallet to that address, in one transaction.
5. Keep the page open, or copy the order link. The page follows the order until it arrives.

To receive on Zcash, use a transparent address (t1… or t3…), and shield it in your Zcash wallet after it arrives.

If an order cannot be completed, the funds go back to your refund address.

## Fees

The quote already includes all fees, including Nullmask's 0.2%. Your wallet pays its usual network fee to send the deposit.
